- Industry: `industry@demo.com`
- Institution: `admin@demo.com`

## Recommended judge journey
1. Login as Student.
2. Open **Career Intelligence**.
3. Change target to **Data Analyst**.
4. Review the AI Career Twin, readiness dimensions, demand radar and ranked gaps.
5. Open **What-If Simulator** and select missing milestones.
6. Click **Build My Roadmap**.
7. Open **Learning** and choose a course, then a specific topic.
8. Watch the embedded subject-specific video, complete Quick Check, complete Practice and update the skill.
9. Return to Career Intelligence: the intelligence snapshot now reflects the persisted profile/learning state.
10. Open Opportunities to show explainable match ranking.
11. Open Portfolio/Passport to show evidence and resume strength.
12. Open Notifications to demonstrate unread/read and mark-all-read behavior.
13. Login as Industry to demonstrate candidate search, opportunity publishing and recruitment pipeline.
14. Login as Institution to demonstrate student/branch/placement analytics.

## Important product message
EduLyra is not another job portal. It connects:

**Education → Skills → Evidence → Learning → Projects → Industry → Experience → Opportunities → Career**

Everything is organized around **One profile. One skill journey.**
